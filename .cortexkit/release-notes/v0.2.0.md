insula 0.2.0 is the first release since 0.1.1 (6 September). It polls 48 AI providers in the background and answers quota, credit and spend questions from its latest readings, without waiting on the network. It needs CortexKit core 0.21.3 or newer.

## Before you upgrade

- **Grant insula access to your vault logins.** insula now reads every login and API key from the vault by its credential id, so new accounts appear without any config. A fresh install needs two read grants:
  `ck auth grant --principal reserved:insula --selector-kind category --selector llm-provider --operation read`
  `ck auth grant --principal reserved:insula --selector-kind category --selector browser-session --operation read`
- **Browser logins now come from the vault only.** insula no longer reads Chrome's or any other browser's cookie store, on any platform, and needs no Full Disk Access or Keychain permission. Providers that read a website (Claude Console, Cursor, Ollama, Langdock and others) use a login deposited as `cookie:<domain>:<account>`, either by an agent's browser capture or with `ck auth put`.
- **Gemini is removed.** Google ended Gemini Code Assist for individual accounts. Google quota is read through the Antigravity provider.

## New providers

- Meta Muse Code (5-hour and weekly windows, with the dev.meta.ai team quota while the 5-hour window is idle)
- Claude Console API credits, per grant with expiry dates
- OpenAI Platform API credits, from a browser session key
- Vercel AI Gateway (credits, and team budgets as a separate provider)
- LiteLLM proxy budgets
- Langdock, OpenDesign, Trae CN, Chatbox AI, abliteration.ai, Soniox
- Cline Pass, OpenCode Go and MiniMax now also read their keys from the vault

## Provider updates

Provider reading matches [CodexBar](https://github.com/steipete/CodexBar), the macOS usage app many of these adapters are translated from, up to its v0.83.0. Highlights:

- **Claude:** saved resets, subscription renewal dates, the weekly usage breakdown, per-model weekly limits such as Fable, and paid extra usage as its own pool. Polling is slower (every 3 minutes, every 15 while reply readings arrive), and a 429 respects `retry-after`.
- **Codex:** workspace credit limits, and prepaid credits with their spend limit.
- **Antigravity:** separate Gemini and Claude/GPT pools, Pro-only buckets such as Gemini 4 Argon, and reads from the vault login without the editor running.
- **Others:** Cursor's on-demand spend and X Subscription weekly pool, Ollama's free plan and credit balance, Kimi's 5-hour, weekly and monthly pools, Qwen Cloud team plans, JetBrains newer log formats, Z.ai prepaid balance, Copilot ended subscriptions, Synthetic's weekly pool.

## Codex saved resets

- Automatic use is **off by default**. When you turn it on in `~/.config/cortexkit/ck-quota.jsonc` (`codex.auto_use_resets`), insula spends a reset only when every Codex account reports its limit reached. It spends at most one every 10 minutes, on the account whose weekly reset is furthest away.
- The CortexKit phone app can redeem a saved reset by hand.
- Config changes apply within about 5 seconds, with no restart.

## For apps and other modules

- `usage.watch` streams a small notice when an account's reading changes.
- Each new reading is published on ck-bus as a `quota_sampled` event.
- `usage.observe` accepts quota readings taken from model replies (from Broca, and from the vault on behalf of auth plugins), so insula can poll less.
- New fields: `windowKind`, `credentialRef`, `savedResets`, subscription dates, and pool `resetsAt`, `expiresAt` and `unlimited`. See `docs/consumer-contract.md`.

## Fixes

- A re-login after a rejected credential shows within about a minute instead of five.
- A vault that hasn't answered yet at start no longer makes accounts look removed.
- A credential with no record version no longer retries every minute while it's failing.
- Readings can't move backwards when an older one arrives late, and float noise in percentages is rounded away.

## Platforms

macOS (Apple silicon, signed and notarized), Linux x64 and Windows x64.
