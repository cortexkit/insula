insula 0.2.1 fixes how a redeemed Codex saved reset shows, and ships on three more platforms. It needs CortexKit core 0.21.3 or newer.

## Fixes

- **A redeemed Codex saved reset shows at once.** After a redeem, insula polls that account straight away. Before, while model replies were reporting the account's quota, the poll could wait up to 15 minutes, so the account kept its pre-reset reading and saved-reset count. The reset itself was always applied.

## Platforms

macOS (Apple silicon and Intel, signed and notarized), Linux (x64 and ARM64) and Windows (x64 and ARM64). macOS Intel, Linux ARM64 and Windows ARM64 are new in this release.
