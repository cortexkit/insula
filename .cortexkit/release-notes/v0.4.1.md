insula 0.4.1 keeps an idle account's quota reading fresh. It needs CortexKit core 0.21.3 or newer.

## Fixes

- **Readings stay fresh after model replies stop.** When an auth plugin or Broca reports quota from model replies, insula holds off polling that account. It used to hold off for up to 15 minutes after its last poll, even once the replies had stopped, so an idle account's reading could be 15 minutes old. Apps and plugins that treat an old reading as missing then polled the provider themselves. Now insula polls again one normal interval after the last reported reading (60 s, or 180 s for Claude).
- **Langdock** sends its `Accept` header once instead of twice.

## Platforms

macOS (Apple silicon and Intel, signed and notarized), Linux (x64 and ARM64) and Windows (x64 and ARM64).
