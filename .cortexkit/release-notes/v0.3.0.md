insula 0.3.0 shows Claude saved resets by type, lets the phone and desktop use one, and adds three providers from CodexBar v0.84.0. It needs CortexKit core 0.21.3 or newer.

## New providers

- **SiliconFlow**, as two providers pinned to their regions: `siliconflow` (International, USD) from `apikey:siliconflow` and `siliconflow-cn` (China, CNY) from `apikey:siliconflow-cn`. A key is sent only to its own region. The account balance is shown exactly, including a negative overdue balance.
- **Tripo** credits from `apikey:tripo`: available credits and frozen reservations as two separate pools.
- **TypeSafe** billing balance from a deposited `cookie:console.typesafe.ai` login. TypeSafe API keys can't read billing, so a browser login is needed.

## Provider updates

- **Claude saved resets by type.** Each Claude account's `savedResets` now lists its resets by kind: `session` (the 5-hour window), `weekly`, `full` (both) and `model_weekly` (one model's own weekly limit, such as Fable). The counts add up to the total.
- **NeuralWatt** shows its reported prepaid dollar balance beside the kWh and key-allowance windows.
- **Synthetic** no longer shows the retired 5-hour allowance beside a readable new weekly plan. Older plans are unchanged.

## For apps and other modules

- `usage.use_saved_reset` accepts `provider: "claude"` with a required `kind`. It is admitted from the same callers as Codex: the phone, and the desktop app once the daemon attests it. One redeem per account per 30 minutes applies, and a retried `request_id` replays its stored answer without spending again. `recently_reset` and `provider_refused` can carry Anthropic's cooldown time and reason code. Samples are tagged `quota-phone-v3`.
- New wire member: `savedResets.kinds`, from `cortexkit-provider-usage` 0.16.0. Readers that don't know it ignore it.

## Platforms

macOS (Apple silicon and Intel, signed and notarized), Linux (x64 and ARM64) and Windows (x64 and ARM64).
